Good questions

Questions parents ask.

Wondering what your teen will build, how the sessions work or what comes next? Start here—or ask us directly.

Ask us anything ↗
Does my teen need to know how to code?

No. The program is designed for beginners, and you’ll get guidance choosing tools and building step by step.

What kind of apps can my teen build?

It could be an app, a business, an AI-powered tool or a project that helps your community. We’ll help you shape an idea you’re excited about.

How long is the program?

The guided program runs for eight weeks, with one-to-one live mentoring and independent project work.

How does the mentoring work?

Students get personalized one-to-one live guidance, with time between sessions to work on their own project.

How much time should I set aside?

Plan for around two to three hours a week, including a one-hour live mentoring session.

Can my teen generate real revenue?

Students learn to build, launch and explore ways to monetize their products. Outcomes vary by project; we’ll help your teen understand the next steps.

What is the program’s success rate?

The current offer includes a results guarantee or your money back. We’ll explain the guarantee terms during a free consultation.

What universities have students been admitted to?

Students have earned admissions to top universities in the US and UK, including Ivy League schools. Admissions decisions are made by universities, and outcomes are not guaranteed.

What ongoing support do you provide?

Your teen can continue to access guidance and the alumni community after the program, as they keep developing their product.

What happens after we get in touch?

We’ll learn about your teen’s interests and idea, talk through the program and help you decide whether it’s a good fit.
